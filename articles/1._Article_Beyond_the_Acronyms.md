Mint Building Services carries a relatively new name in the region. The business behind it has been delivering projects since 2010. A management buyout in 2022 laid the foundation for its next stage, driven by the commitment and determination of the leadership team. Since then, the company has expanded sector coverage and regional presence, broadened its technical capability, and established a clear framework for disciplined growth across Southeast Asia.

We are an M&E contractor

Walk onto any live construction site in Southeast Asia and ask for the M&E contractor, and you will be pointed in the right direction. The term is familiar and widely understood. However, mechanical and electrical systems no longer operate in isolation. Building performance now depends on how these systems are integrated to deliver reliable, efficient operation over time.

Building Services is a more accurate reflection of this role. Rather than describing individual trades, it refers to the full network of systems required for a building to function properly. Successful project outcomes are no longer measured by the quality of installation alone, but by how well systems are coordinated and how they perform over time.

Client expectations have also shifted. Projects increasingly require a single partner who understands both construction delivery and how a building will operate in practice. The term Building Services reflects this broader scope and level of responsibility.

We Deliver Building Services

Core systems such as MV and LV distribution, ductwork and pipework are delivered in a traditional manner, using our own workforce and direct procurement of materials. This provides control over quality, programme and cost across the core installation works.

As the project moves into full system installation, the scope shifts from individual trades to integrated delivery. Systems such as lighting controls, BMS integration, and fire alarm and sprinkler modifications must be managed and coordinated as a single package.

ICT systems sit alongside this. Our in-house technology division manages and coordinates client vendors for IT, AV and security, ensuring alignment with the wider building systems.

Together, this is what we define as Building Services. It is an approach we have delivered for more than 15 years across the commercial, hospitality and retail sectors.

Building Regional Capability

As demand grew for contractors capable of delivering comprehensive building services packages, a clear opportunity emerged to expand beyond Singapore into Malaysia and Thailand, enabling Mint to support clients on a regional basis.

The company’s early focus centred on commercial office environments and workplace fit-outs, where coordination, programme management and cost control are fundamental to successful delivery. Over time, projects expanded in both scale and complexity: from multi-phase hospitality developments in Singapore to flagship retail environments in Malaysia and industrial facilities in Thailand.

Today, with the continued growth of mission-critical facilities across the region, Mint’s capability to deliver projects across the full building services spectrum provides a strong platform for what comes next. The name says it plainly. The work confirms it.
