Editor’s letter
Azryl Ali · Editor of MINDMEP
Welcome to the very first issue of MINDMEP. We turned a simple capability statement into a magazine: we mapped the threads between projects, people and ideas like a mind map, then paired it with our core discipline, MEP.

MINDMEP connects how we think with what we build.

We also designed it to look fabulous on a coffee table. The A5 format sits neatly in reception or the living room, quiet yet bold, ready to be picked up and talked about.

On our cover, we spotlight one of the heritage buildings Mint is currently working on, a project that embodies the delicate balance between preserving history and enabling modern function.

In our feature article, Old Bones, New Beats, we take you inside Mint’s growing capability in conservation engineering and the challenges of working within Singapore’s heritage landscape.

This issue also introduces several key leaders in new roles: Shan Nathan, General Manager for Singapore and Malaysia; Nuno Rio Tinto, who now leads in Thailand; and Jaymee Recto, our Commercial Director.

For me personally, it has been a joy working alongside Mint Building Services and Mark Donlon over the past two years.

Thank you for reading this debut issue. I hope you find it as engaging to explore as it has been to put together.
Azryl Ali
