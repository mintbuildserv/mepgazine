Money is on site

Speed sharpened her instincts at ISG and TCG. Huawei rollouts were the testing ground. “You win those jobs during procurement by involving the site team early. Things move faster when procurement is informed by how the work will really be done.”

The QA/QC lens never left either. “Quality delivered on time is what leaves a mark. Get those two right and clients come back.” She also rejects the myth that QS work is desk bound.

Moving to Mint Building Services completed the loop. She had learned shell and core, then fit-out. What she wanted was the part that makes a building work.

Building services turns a structure into a system of power, plant, and control. It is also a different commercial seat. As a specialist, Mint must be nimble and exact, aligning design with means and methods while protecting margin.

Success now feels shared. Big contractors often measure by individual KPI. At Mint, small wins are noticed and celebrated. Repeatable delivery, tidy close-outs, and healthy cash are the markers.

For Jaymee, cost management is how agility and quality become real. Deliver on time, with quality, within budget. Give clients best value, not just the lowest line on a spreadsheet.

She backs two habits that reduce risk: structured cost and time reviews at start, mid-point and pre-close, and rigorous use of BIM and reliable supply chains to surface clashes early and lock lead times.

Some commercial edges stay in the playbook, but early alignment on scope, temporary systems and testing keeps disputes down.

Outside work she resets with music, art and books. Late-night cost plans run on coffee, black and hot. Favourite buildings: the Orchard Road malls for commercial complexity, and the Fullerton Hotel for beauty.

Advice to her 22-year-old self: enjoy every moment and every opportunity. Asked about the move to Mint, she is already sure it was worth it.

Looking ahead she sees a busy decade. “Construction in 2035 will be massive,” she says. Revamps and regulatory updates will drive the work.

The future belongs to those who think clearly, act early, and keep the numbers honest on site.
