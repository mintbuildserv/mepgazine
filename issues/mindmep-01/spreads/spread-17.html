Shan has been there from day one. Back when Mint was still Update Asia. Back when the team was small, the hustle was real, and nothing moved without a hundred hands, and probably a few late-night coffees.

But what made him stay through the transition wasn’t nostalgia. It was trust. In Mark. In the vision. And in the chance to build, not just buildings, but a business.

“I didn’t plan to become a General Manager,” he says. “I just focused on delivering projects.” But growth has a funny way of finding those who keep showing up.

One day you’re knee-deep in coordination drawings; the next, you’re steering operations across two countries.

Shan’s background in electrical engineering and MEP coordination wasn’t something he left behind when he moved into management. It is what shapes his leadership today: practical, grounded, and unshaken by pressure.
