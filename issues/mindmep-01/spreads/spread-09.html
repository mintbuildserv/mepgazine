Core systems, delivered direct

MV and LV distribution, ductwork and pipework are delivered in a traditional manner, using our own workforce and direct procurement of materials. This gives us control over quality, programme and cost across the core installation works.

Integrated delivery

As a project moves into full system installation, the scope shifts from individual trades to integrated delivery. Systems such as lighting controls, BMS integration, and fire alarm and sprinkler modifications must be managed and coordinated as a single package.

Technology in house

ICT systems sit alongside this. Our in-house technology division manages and coordinates client vendors for IT, AV and security, ensuring alignment with the wider building systems.
