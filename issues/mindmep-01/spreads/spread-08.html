Beyond the acronyms

Mint Building Services carries a relatively new name in the region. The business behind it has been delivering projects since 2010. A management buyout in 2022 laid the foundation for its next stage, driven by the commitment and determination of the leadership team. Since then, the company has expanded its sector coverage and regional presence, broadened its technical capability, and established a clear framework for disciplined growth across Southeast Asia.

We are an M&E contractor

Walk onto any live construction site in Southeast Asia and ask for the M&E contractor, and you will be pointed in the right direction. The term is familiar and widely understood.

But mechanical and electrical systems no longer operate in isolation. Building performance now depends on how these systems are integrated to deliver reliable, efficient operation over time.

Building Services is a more accurate reflection of this role. Rather than describing individual trades, it refers to the full network of systems required for a building to function.

Successful project outcomes are no longer measured by the quality of installation alone, but by how well systems are coordinated and how they perform once handed over.

Client expectations have shifted with it. Projects increasingly require a single partner who understands both construction delivery and how a building will operate in practice.

The term Building Services reflects that broader scope and level of responsibility.
