Building regional capability

As demand grew for contractors capable of delivering comprehensive building services packages, a clear opportunity emerged to expand beyond Singapore into Malaysia and Thailand. This enabled Mint to support clients on a regional basis.

The company’s early focus centred on commercial office environments and workplace fit-outs, where coordination, programme management and cost control are fundamental to successful delivery.

Over time, projects expanded in both scale and complexity: from multi-phase hospitality developments in Singapore to flagship retail environments in Malaysia and industrial facilities in Thailand.

Today, with continued growth in mission-critical facilities across the region, Mint’s capability to deliver across the full building services spectrum provides a strong platform for what comes next.

Mint in Thailand

Mint has entered Thailand with clear intent: bring our trusted MEPF delivery model to Bangkok and build lasting client relationships through speed, quality and accountability. The launch is anchored by our first completed project in the Kingdom, YETI’s APAC workplace, delivered on a 14-week programme with full MEPF scope.

Our Thailand team pairs local subcontractors and suppliers with Mint’s design-assist and buildability mindset, so decisions are made early and procurement matches how the job will actually be built.
